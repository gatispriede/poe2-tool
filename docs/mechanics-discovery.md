# Mechanics discovery sweep — uniques · tree · ascendancy · supports

Generated by `node scripts/discover-mechanics.js` (re-run any time data
refreshes). The sweep mines every data source for wordings that ADD or CHANGE
a mechanic on top of a base skill — triggers, conversions, additional actions,
replacements, ailment behaviour — as opposed to pure stat boosts.

**Sweep totals:** 1,609 raw mechanic lines → **931 distinct mechanics**.

| Bucket | Distinct | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| support-id | 221 | support-gem behaviours (PoB stat ids) |
| trigger | 133 | "cast/trigger X on hit/crit/kill/…" |
| grant-skill | 120 | items/nodes that grant or socket a skill |
| conversion | 98 | damage-type conversion / gain-as-extra |
| replacement | 90 | "instead / cannot / always" — rewrites a rule |
| ailment-mech | 75 | shock/freeze/ignite/poison behaviour changes |
| per-stat | 74 | "per X" scaling engines |
| resource-mech | 69 | charge / mana / life / leech mechanics |
| multiplier-mech | 27 | lucky/double/twice |
| extra-action | 24 | +projectile/chain/pierce/cascade |

This is the raw vocabulary for discovery. Below: the highest-value mechanics
and — more importantly — the **novel cross-source combinations** they enable.

## A. The 33 keystones (build-defining replacements)

Keystones are the biggest single-node mechanic swings. The offence/identity ones:

- **Avatar of Fire** — 75% of all damage → fire, deal no non-fire. Enables a
  pure-fire conversion build from any base damage type.
- **Blackflame Covenant** — Fire Spells convert fire→chaos; ignite deals chaos
  instead of fire. **Ignite that ignores resistance** (see combo §F2).
- **Eldritch Battery** — ES→mana (fuels Archmage). Already modelled.
- **Blood Magic** — no mana, costs paid from life. Frees the mana pool.
- **Mind Over Matter** — damage taken from mana before life (defensive layer).
- **Resolute Technique** — double accuracy, never crit (anti-crit determinism).
- **Pain Attunement** — +30% more crit damage on low life (crit payoff).
- **Chaos Inoculation** — life = 1, immune to chaos (CI ES builds).
- **Whispers of Doom** — +1 curse (stacks with Lich, §F4).
- **Resonance** — rotates charge types (power→frenzy→endurance).
- **Elemental Equilibrium** — rotates infusion remnant types.
- **Iron Reflexes / Eternal Youth / Zealot's Oath / Vaal Pact** — defence rewrites.

Full list: `node scripts/discover-mechanics.js` → REPLACEMENT bucket.

## B. Trigger engines (free skills off your main action)

The core of indirect-damage discovery — fire a payload skill without casting it:

- **Choir of the Storm** (unique) — Trigger Lightning Bolt on Critical Hit.
- **Stormweaver · Tempest Caller** (asc) — Trigger Elemental Storm on Crit with Spells.
- **Smith of Kitava · Heat of the Forge** (asc) — grants Fire Spell on Hit (no crit needed).
- **Invoker · …Scatter to the Winds** (asc) — Trigger Elemental Expression on Melee Crit.
- **Amazon · Elemental Surge** (asc) — Trigger on Charge consume.
- **Corpsewade** (unique) — Trigger Decompose every 1.2m travelled (movement trigger).
- **Choir / The Last Lament** — load bolts / triggers on reload (crossbow rhythm).
- Plus ~120 "Used when you become Ignited/Shocked/Poisoned/Stunned" charm-trigger lines.

These are alternatives to the Spark→Cast-on-Crit→Comet chain — many don't need
crit (Heat of the Forge fires on hit), opening lower-investment trigger builds.

## C. Crit-source discoveries (directly unblocks build #35)

The crit-build blocker was "can't reach the meta's 367% crit". The sweep found
crit sources that DON'T come from crit-chance gear/tree:

- **Amazon · Critical Strike** (asc) — *"Chance to Hit with Attacks can exceed
  100%"* + *"Gain additional Critical Hit Chance equal to 25% of excess chance
  to Hit"*. **Accuracy-stacking becomes crit chance.** A whole crit build
  fuelled by accuracy, sidestepping the crit-chance allocation ceiling.
- **Pain Attunement** (keystone) — +30% more crit damage bonus on low life.
- **Resolute Technique** (keystone) — the anti-crit option (double accuracy,
  never crit) for builds that can't reach crit thresholds — the deterministic
  alternative the generator should also try.

The Amazon accuracy→crit path is the concrete answer to the crit-discovery
problem: model "excess accuracy → crit" and a crit build emerges from accuracy.

## D. Conversion engines (the resistance-sidestep, §user's chaos point)

Converting elemental → chaos means the hit ignores the 50% boss elemental
resistance (chaos res is 0 on bosses):

- **Voltaxic Rift** (unique) — 100% Lightning → Chaos. A lightning build that
  pays no resistance tax on bosses.
- **Blackflame Covenant** (keystone) — Fire Spells fire→chaos; ignite→chaos.
- **Acolyte of Chayula · Damage as Chaos** (asc) — gain 4% of damage as extra chaos.
- **Painter's Servant** (unique) — 33% elemental → each of cold/fire/lightning
  (spreads a single element across all three — multi-element scaling).
- **Heatshiver** (unique) — gain cold as extra fire PER chill magnitude on enemy
  (chill-stacking → fire damage; a cold→fire feedback engine).
- **Invoker · I am the Thunder / Blizzard** (asc) — gain 10% as extra lightning / cold.
- **Doomfletch** (unique) — 100% of phys as extra of EACH element (triple-dip).

## E2. Defence-as-offence — ARMOUR AS DAMAGE (a top archetype the first sweep missed)

The original sweep missed this whole class because the wording is *"% of Armour
**also applies to** Damage"* — not "converted to" / "gain as", and "armour"
reads as a defensive stat. The `defence-as-dmg` classifier bucket now catches it
(30 distinct). It's a full archetype: **your defensive armour pool becomes flat
hit damage.**

- **Doryani's Prototype** (body) — `+100% of Armour applies to Lightning Damage`
  + *"Enemies in your Presence have Lightning Resistance equal to yours"* +
  *"Lightning Resistance does not affect Lightning damage taken"*. Stack
  **negative** lightning res on yourself → enemies inherit it → you
  over-penetrate, and you take no lightning damage for it.
- **Smith of Kitava** (Warrior asc) — the armour-as-damage ascendancy:
  - `Smith's Masterwork`: **+200 Armour per connected notable allocated** (the
    tree itself becomes a giant armour pool).
  - `Flowing Metal`: Body Armour grants **+50% of Armour applies to Elemental**.
  - `Dedication to Kitava`: Body Armour grants **+100% of Armour applies to
    CHAOS Damage** — armour→chaos **ignores boss resistance**.
  - `Heat of the Forge`: grants Fire Spell on Hit (a payload for the flat).
- **Tree notables**: Heatproof/Chillproof/Shockproof/Prism Guard/Tempered
  Defences — `+15–30% of Armour applies to <type>` each. Plus Heavy Armour
  (Str requirements → armour) and Strength stacking (Str → armour).

**The standout combo:** Smith of Kitava + heavy armour stacking + Dedication to
Kitava → **armour → chaos damage**. A defensive stat becomes *unresisted* boss
damage, on a class that's inherently tanky. This is the user's target build.

**GENERATOR (v18b — committed archetype).** Added an `armour` scaling bias that
routes Warrior to **Smith of Kitava** and stacks armour + armour-applies nodes,
generated alongside balanced/crit/ailment and ranked by composed DPS. Two
realism guards were required: (1) `computeArmour` is PER-ITEM (quality and
"% increased Armour" are local to their item — summing across ~15 items gave a
1350% bug → every build equipped Doryani's and ballooned 11×); (2) armour-as-
damage uniques (Doryani's Prototype) are excluded from the gear pool unless the
build is armour-committed, so they don't bolt a type-mismatched flat onto bow/
spell builds. Verified: Huntress back to its 0.93M baseline, the Warrior armour
build still surfaces. KNOWN: numbers carry the usual generator optimism, and
armour-applied damage shares the dominant type's increased scaling (per-type is
the refinement).

**MODELLED (v18).** `maxArmour.computeArmour` aggregates the pool (base gear
armour + flat "+N to Armour" + % increased + quality); `aggregateMods` parses
"+X% of Armour also applies to <type> Damage" per destination; the composer
adds `armour × sum%` as flat hit damage (attack and spell), and the chaos
portion's fraction of the hit is credited as unresisted via the resistance
blend. Verified: 17.9k armour + armour→chaos on a pinnacle → effectiveness
×0.98 (the hit is mostly unresisted chaos). REFINEMENTS QUEUED: Strength→armour,
Smith's "+200 per connected notable", determination-style auras, and per-type
resistance for the armour-elemental portion (currently shares the dominant
type). Magnitude is chassis-dependent — a real Warrior has a higher armour pool
but lower crit/increased multipliers than the Ice Shot chassis used in tests.

Also surfaced in this bucket: **Armour Break** (Warbringer "Break Armour = 10%
of hit" + Imploding Impacts "broken armour increases all damage taken" — an
enemy debuff independent of your damage type) and **Thorns** (Revenge: thorns =
10% of item armour; Icetomb: cold thorns from mana).

## F. NOVEL CROSS-SOURCE COMBINATIONS (the actual discoveries)

These chain mechanics from different sources into builds nobody templated:

1. **Accuracy-crit trigger** — Amazon (excess accuracy → crit) + Choir of the
   Storm (Lightning Bolt on crit). Stack accuracy → high crit → constant free
   Lightning Bolts. A crit build with zero crit-chance gear.

2. **Chaos ignite that ignores boss res** — Blackflame Covenant (ignite deals
   chaos) + fire-stacking + Pathfinder/ignite scaling. A boss-DoT that pays no
   resistance tax. Beats elemental ignite into 50% res.

3. **Lightning→chaos crit caster** — Voltaxic Rift (lightning→chaos) +
   Stormweaver crit/shock + Archmage (lightning gain-as). Archmage's flat
   lightning becomes chaos → full damage on bosses despite the lightning base.

4. **Triple curse** — Whispers of Doom (keystone, +1) + Lich · Incessant
   Cacophony (asc, +1) = 3 curses. Curse-stacking caster.

5. **Cold→fire chill-feedback** — Heatshiver (cold as extra fire per chill) +
   freeze/chill stacking (Stormweaver/cold tree). Chill magnitude scales your
   fire damage — a hybrid cold/fire engine.

6. **Movement-trigger clear** — Corpsewade (Decompose every 1.2m) + high
   movement speed → continuous AoE while running. Zero-button clear.

7. **On-hit free spell** — Smith of Kitava · Heat of the Forge (Fire Spell on
   Hit) + a fast multi-hit attack (Lightning Arrow chains) → free fire spell
   per chain hit, no crit needed. Lower-investment than Cast-on-Crit.

## Modelling status & next steps for the engine

Of these, the composer currently models: gain-as-extra (collapsed), conversion
(partial), Archmage, Spell Cascade, Cast-on-Crit, max charges, ailment
reliability, enemy resistance + penetration. NOT yet modelled (priority for
turning these discoveries into ranked builds):

1. ~~**Conversion-to-chaos resistance bypass**~~ — **DONE (v14, source-aware).**
   `damageEffectiveness` blends chaos effectiveness for the converted fraction;
   `aggregateMods` parses Voltaxic ("X% of Lightning Damage Converted to Chaos")
   and Blackflame ("Convert X% of Fire Damage to Chaos") AND captures the
   conversion's SOURCE type. The composer credits the bypass only when the
   source matches the hit's dominant type — so Voltaxic (lightning→chaos) does
   nothing to a cold Ice Shot (which is correct, and stops the generator from
   faking a bypass by equipping Voltaxic on a cold build). Verified: lightning
   vs 50%-res pinnacle = ×0.50 raw → ×1.00 converted. KNOWN LIMITATION: a true
   multi-step chain (cold→lightning→chaos via Whisper+Voltaxic) isn't credited
   because our single-dominant-type model can't track the cold→lightning
   intermediate — flagged in the composer notes. Tracking conversion-driven
   dominant-type shifts is the per-type-damage refinement.
2. ~~**Accuracy → crit (Amazon)**~~ — **DONE (v15), provisional formula.**
   `aggregateMods` parses the Amazon node ("Critical Hit Chance equal to 25% of
   excess chance to Hit") + accuracy rating; the composer credits
   `excess × 25%` to crit chance, flag-gated so non-Amazon builds keep the
   100%-hit assumption (no regression). Enemy evasion added to profiles
   (pinnacle 1175, ground truth). The excess-hit FORMULA (accuracy beyond the
   95%-hit threshold) is a transparent, calibratable proxy — surfaced in the
   composer's notes as PROVISIONAL; needs a real Amazon accuracy-crit fixture
   to tune. The plumbing (parse → accuracy aggregation → crit contribution) is
   complete and correct; only the magnitude curve is provisional.
3. **Standalone trigger gems** as first-class (task #20) — Choir / Heat of the
   Forge / Tempest Caller as trigger sources beyond Spark-CoC.
4. **Curse/ailment-stack multipliers** — additional-curse and double-poison
   effects as damage multipliers.

These four turn the discovered mechanics into composer-rankable builds. Each is
scoped in its own task.
